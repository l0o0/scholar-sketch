# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers and knowledge workers who organize literature in Zotero and want to
write, connect, and exchange durable research material without leaving their
desktop workflow. They work with both Chinese and Latin text, often across
Markdown, Zotero items, PDF annotations, and AI-assisted tools.

## Product Purpose

Scholar Sketch makes Markdown and spatial research canvases native to Zotero. It keeps
research material portable and inspectable while providing a focused surface for
turning sources, excerpts, notes, questions, and claims into connected thinking.
Success means users can move between collecting, reading, and writing without
losing provenance or being forced into a proprietary document model.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and native. The interface should feel like a dependable research
desk: compact enough for frequent use, calm enough for long sessions, and clear
about the distinction between source material and the user's own thinking.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like card grids, decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized
shadows, marketing composition, noisy status chrome, and controls that compete
with the research content. Do not imitate an IDE or turn semantic object types
into a dense form-filling workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Keep research content and provenance legible at a glance.
2. Let standard Zotero and canvas interactions remain familiar.
3. Prefer a small, composable object vocabulary over feature-specific types.
4. Preserve portable data and explicit user intent across every transformation.
5. Add structure progressively, without burdening ordinary human editing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep all essential actions keyboard-operable, provide visible focus and readable
contrast in light and dark themes, avoid relying on color alone, respect reduced
motion, and preserve clear Chinese and Latin typography and localization.
